02 / Our story

A Korean word.
A simple idea.

알차다 · alchada

In Korean, 알차다 describes something substantial — well-filled, thoughtful, and worth more than its size suggests.

That's where alcha gets its name. We believe good software doesn't have to be complicated to be valuable. It should feel effortless on the outside and carefully considered within.

Our philosophy: make room for what matters, and make every detail count.
